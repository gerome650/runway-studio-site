Process

Approve ten seconds before we make sixty.

AI generation is not repeatable by default. The process is built so that you see and sign off the look before production volume begins.

  1. 01

    Brief

    Send a brief, a board, or a handful of phone photos. We work from whatever you have.

  2. 02

    Alignment

    Character sheets, storyboards and look references, agreed before anything is generated.

  3. 03

    Sample

    A ten-second sample in the final look. You approve it, or we adjust it.

  4. 04

    Production

    Generation, a shoot where the brief needs one, then edit, grade and sound.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Masters in every ratio, with a record of how each asset was made and the usage rights that apply.
